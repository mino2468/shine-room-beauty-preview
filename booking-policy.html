YOUR APPOINTMENT

Booking & cancellation

Simple arrangements that help the studio give every appointment its time. Updated 12 November 2026.

Booking your visit

Appointments are booked through Booksy. Choose your service and an available date and time, then check the details before confirming. Booksy shows current availability and the information attached to your booking. If you need help choosing, contact Aneta before you book.

Book on Booksy ↗

No deposit

Shine Room Beauty currently does not require a deposit to book your appointment.

Please give 24 hours’ notice

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please do so at least 24 hours before your appointment. This gives the studio time to offer the appointment to someone else. You can make the change in Booksy, call 089 200 4741 or send a WhatsApp message to the same number.

If something unexpected happens within 24 hours of your visit, contact the studio as soon as you can.

Arriving at the studio

Please arrive at your booked time. If you expect to be late or cannot find the studio, call so Aneta knows. The studio is at First Floor Unit, Prosperous Road, Clane, Co. Kildare, W91 AV2C.

Choosing a suitable treatment

A booking does not by itself establish that a treatment is suitable for every person. Relevant suitability, preparation and aftercare should be discussed with Aneta. Ask before booking if you are uncertain. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed by photographs or descriptions on this website.

Questions or changes?

Contact the studio by phone or WhatsApp on +353 89 200 4741. Opening a WhatsApp link does not send a message until you choose to send it.
