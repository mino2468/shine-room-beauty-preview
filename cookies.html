Cookies Policy

A clear explanation of what this website stores and when external content is loaded. Updated 13 November 2026.

Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are one way websites can store information on your device. Local storage is another. This website uses local storage to remember choices you make. It does not use its own analytics or advertising trackers.

What this site stores

shine-language

Purpose: remembers the Polish or English language you choose manually. Location: local storage in your browser. Duration: until you change the choice, clear the site’s stored data or your browser removes it. The automatic initial language selection can work without saving a preference.

shine-privacy-choice

Purpose: remembers your privacy choice, including whether the optional Google Map is allowed. Location: local storage in your browser. Duration: until you change the choice, clear the site’s stored data or your browser removes it. It is used to apply your settings, not to follow your activity across websites.

Optional Google Maps

The homepage and Contact page offer an embedded Google Map. Maps stay disabled unless you allow them in the privacy controls or choose to load a map on either page. Your browser then connects to Google, which may use its own cookies, local storage or other technologies under its own policies. These external technologies are not controlled or used by Shine Room Beauty for website analytics.

The map is optional: refusing it does not prevent you from reading the address, using this website, calling the studio or booking through Booksy. A separate Google Maps directions link opens Google only when you follow that link.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Select Cookie settings in the footer to review your preferences at any time. You can turn optional maps off there. The site then removes any embedded map it has loaded. Removing a map does not itself erase cookies Google may already have placed; use your browser settings to remove those if you wish.

You can also clear all data stored by this website in your browser. The site may then ask for your preferences again. If your browser blocks local storage, the website can still be read, but choices may not remain saved between visits.

External links and more information

Booksy, WhatsApp and Instagram are linked services, not automatically loaded social feeds. When you open one of them, its own policies apply. To ask about this website’s privacy choices, contact Shine Room Beauty on +353 89 200 4741.
