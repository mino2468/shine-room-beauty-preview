01 / Skin & facials

Skin & facials

HydraFacial, Dermapen and facial care selected around your skin.

HydraFacial

€100

Device-led cleansing, gentle suction and hydrating skin care.

How it works

HydraFacial combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration in one device-led facial. A handpiece passes over the skin while treatment solutions loosen surface build-up, and controlled suction helps lift impurities from pores. Hydrating ingredients complete the treatment, leaving the focus on a fresher, smoother-looking complexion. Before your appointment, Aneta discusses your skin and chooses the appropriate care. Products and any additional stages are selected individually, so the treatment reflects your skin’s needs and response.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Discuss skin needs and cleanse the area.
  2. Exfoliate and remove impurities using the treatment handpiece.
  3. Finish with hydration and individual aftercare advice.

Your skin response and the care recommended afterwards are individual.

Facial treatments

from €100

A choice of facial appointments for different skin-care goals.

How it works

Our facial menu offers different approaches to cleansing, hydration and a more even-looking complexion. Cavitation peeling uses a vibrating ultrasonic spatula on damp skin to loosen surface debris and dead skin cells. This facial exfoliation is different from body cavitation. Pink Glow, BB Glow and CytoCare 502 are also listed as separate appointments. Aneta helps you choose the right option and explains its products, application method and aftercare before booking, as these vary between services.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Choose a specific facial after discussing your skin.
  2. Cleanse and carry out the agreed treatment stages.
  3. Review the skin response and the care to follow at home.

Cavitation peeling

An ultrasonic spatula moves over damp skin to help remove surface debris and loosen dead skin cells. This is facial exfoliation, not fat reduction or body cavitation.

Pink Glow

A separate complexion-care appointment. Ask Aneta about the proposed formula, application method and whether it suits your skin before booking.

BB Glow

A complexion-evening treatment category associated with tinted serums. Confirm the product, application method and expected duration with Aneta before booking.

CytoCare 502

A formulation containing hyaluronic acid and a rejuvenating complex, intended to support skin hydration and quality. Discuss its application method, suitability and risks with Aneta first.

The procedure, preparation and aftercare depend on the facial you choose.

Dermapen / microneedling

€150

Fine-needle skin stimulation to address texture and the appearance of scars.

How it works

Microneedling uses a pen-shaped device with fine needles to make controlled, tiny punctures in the skin. This stimulates the skin’s repair response and can improve the appearance of texture, fine lines and some scars over time. Aneta assesses the area and selects the treatment intensity around your skin. Temporary redness and sensitivity can occur afterwards, so preparation and aftercare are an important part of the appointment. Results develop gradually and vary from person to person.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Assess and prepare the skin for the agreed treatment.
  2. Move the fine-needle device across the selected area.
  3. Explain expected sensitivity, recovery and aftercare.

The skin response and any further treatment plan are assessed individually.

02 / Laser & IPL

Laser & IPL

Hair removal, skin-focused light treatments and tattoo removal.

Laser hair removal

Confirm with Aneta

Hair-reduction appointments for selected face and body areas.

How it works

Laser hair removal directs light towards pigment in the hair. The absorbed energy becomes heat and acts on the follicle to reduce future growth. A handpiece treats the chosen area in sections, with protective eyewear and settings selected for your skin and hair. Hairs pass through different growth stages, so a course of appointments is usually discussed. Aneta confirms preparation, the area and the regular price beforehand. Hair colour, skin tone and the treatment area influence the result.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Assess the area, skin, hair and relevant treatment history.
  2. Use protective eyewear and treat the agreed area with light pulses.
  3. Discuss skin care and the timing of any further appointments.

The response and number of appointments vary. No fixed result is promised.

IPL skin rejuvenation

Confirm with Aneta

Light-based skin treatment following a suitability discussion.

How it works

IPL delivers filtered pulses of intense light across a range of wavelengths, rather than a laser’s particular wavelength. Pigment or small blood vessels absorb selected light and convert it to heat, helping address the appearance of uneven colour or visible redness. The handpiece is placed against the agreed skin area while your eyes are protected. Aneta assesses skin tone, tanning and the concern you want to address before selecting a treatment. IPL and laser services are different options, chosen individually.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Discuss the skin concern, tanning and light sensitivity.
  2. Protect the eyes and apply selected light pulses to the agreed area.
  3. Review aftercare and the skin response before planning further visits.

Suitability and the response depend on the individual skin concern and treatment selected.

Tattoo removal

Confirm with Aneta

An individual assessment of the tattoo and treatment area.

How it works

Laser tattoo removal uses short light pulses to break tattoo pigment into smaller fragments, which the body can gradually clear. Aneta assesses the tattoo’s colours, size, location and previous treatments before discussing a plan. The agreed area is treated with protective eyewear, followed by time for the skin to heal between appointments. Fading happens gradually and several visits may be needed. Different inks and skin tones respond differently, and complete removal without skin changes cannot be guaranteed.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Assess the tattoo’s colours, size, location and treatment history.
  2. Protect the eyes and treat the agreed tattoo area.
  3. Explain wound care, healing and review before another session.

The response is individual; complete removal or a particular number of sessions is not guaranteed.

03 / Brows & permanent makeup

Brows & permanent makeup

Powder Brows shaped around your face and preferences.

Powder Brows

€299

Permanent makeup with shape and colour chosen around you.

How it works

Powder Brows uses a permanent-makeup machine to place tiny dots of pigment into the skin, creating soft, powder-like shading rather than individual hair strokes. Aneta discusses and maps the shape and colour around your face, existing brows and preferences before treatment. Brows may look stronger immediately afterwards, then soften as the skin heals. The healed appearance and pigment retention vary individually. Touch-ups and later colour refreshes are booked separately, with their timing discussed around your result.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Discuss, map and agree the proposed brow shape and colour.
  2. Build soft shading with fine dots of pigment.
  3. Explain healing, aftercare and any separately booked touch-up.

The final appearance is individual. Discuss healing, aftercare and follow-up before treatment.

04 / Body contouring

Body contouring

Cavitation, radiofrequency and vacuum massage options.

Cavitation liposuction

Confirm with Aneta

A non-surgical body-treatment option for selected areas.

How it works

Body cavitation uses ultrasound to work on localised body contours without surgery. A handpiece moves over the chosen area with contact gel, delivering sound-wave energy through the skin towards underlying tissue. Unlike surgical liposuction, it involves no incision and does not suction fat out of the body. Aneta assesses the area and discusses whether the treatment suits your goals. It is not a weight-loss treatment, and individual responses vary rather than following a fixed centimetre reduction.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Assess the chosen area and confirm that the treatment is suitable.
  2. Apply contact gel and move the ultrasound handpiece over the area.
  3. Review the response and discuss any proposed follow-up.

Any treatment plan and response are individual; a specific change in measurements is not promised.

Body contouring

from €100

Multipolar RF and vacuum massage with radio waves.

How it works

Choose between two separately bookable body treatments: Multipolar RF or vacuum massage with radio waves. Surface RF sends radiofrequency energy through a handpiece to warm tissue and address the appearance of firmness and skin texture. Vacuum massage adds suction, gently lifting tissue into the applicator as it moves over the area. Aneta helps select the suitable option and treatment area. These non-surgical treatments are not weight-loss methods; responses vary and some effects may be temporary.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Choose RF or vacuum massage with RF after an assessment.
  2. Treat the selected area with the agreed handpiece technique.
  3. Review comfort, skin response and any further plan.

Multipolar RF

A surface applicator uses radiofrequency energy to create controlled tissue warming. This body treatment does not involve microneedling.

Vacuum massage with radio waves

Suction lifts a fold of tissue into a moving applicator, combining a mechanical massage action with RF warming. Intensity and the treated area are agreed individually.

Discuss your desired outcome and suitability; individual responses vary.

05 / Dermal fillers

Dermal fillers

Lip and facial-area options, following an individual assessment.

Dermal fillers

from €150

Lip and facial-area options selected after consultation.

How it works

Dermal fillers use injections to place a gel-like material into selected tissue, adding volume or refining contour in the lips or facial areas. The menu includes lips, chin, cheeks, jaw and full-face options, each priced separately. Before treatment, Aneta discusses your goals, the proposed product, amount and technique, together with aftercare. Swelling and bruising can occur, and uncommon complications can be serious. A suitability assessment and informed consent come before any treatment decision.

The appointment, step by step

  1. Discuss goals, the proposed product, suitability and risks.
  2. Prepare the area and perform the individually agreed injections.
  3. Review the area and provide aftercare and follow-up guidance.

Results, risks and aftercare must be discussed individually before treatment.
