Meet Aneta.

The person behind Shine Room Beauty. A personal approach, a calm studio and time to talk about what matters to you.

ANETA MACKIEWICZ

Care starts with listening.

Shine Room Beauty is Aneta Mackiewicz’s personal beauty studio in Clane, Co. Kildare. The offer brings together skin care, laser treatments, permanent makeup and body treatments.

Before choosing a treatment, there is space to talk about your preferences, your comfort and the result you would like to discuss. The aim is a considered choice that feels right for you.

If you are visiting for the first time or are unsure which service to book, send Aneta a message. You can start with a question, without committing to a particular procedure.
