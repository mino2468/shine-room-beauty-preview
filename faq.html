BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Questions, answered.

A few useful details before you book. For anything personal, message Aneta or call the studio.

How do I book an appointment?

Use Booksy to choose a treatment and see available dates and times. If you would like help choosing a treatment first, contact Aneta on WhatsApp or by phone.

Open Booksy ↗

Is a deposit required?

No deposit is currently required to book an appointment at Shine Room Beauty.

How do I cancel or change my appointment?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Use Booksy, call 089 200 4741 or send a WhatsApp message. If something unexpected happens closer to your appointment, contact the studio as soon as you can.

What if I am not sure which treatment to choose?

Start with a conversation. Tell Aneta what you would like to focus on and what you hope to achieve. Your preferences, suitability and expectations can be discussed before you decide to proceed.

How should I prepare for my visit?

Preparation depends on the treatment. Contact Aneta before your appointment for the guidance relevant to your booking, especially if you have questions about your skin, current products or recent treatments. There is no single preparation routine for every service.

Will my result look exactly like a portfolio photograph?

Portfolio photographs show individual results. Outcomes differ between people and cannot be guaranteed. The treatment, your starting point and your expectations should be discussed before your appointment.

Where is Shine Room Beauty?

The studio is at First Floor Unit, Prosperous Road, Clane, Co. Kildare, W91 AV2C. You will find directions and an optional Google Map on the Contact page.

Find the studio ↗

When is the studio open?

Wednesday to Friday: 09:00–19:00. Saturday: 09:00–14:00. The studio is closed Sunday to Tuesday. Booksy shows the available appointment times.
