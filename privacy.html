Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit this website or contact the studio. Updated 12 November 2026.

Who is responsible?

Shine Room Beauty by Aneta Mackiewicz is responsible for the personal information it receives through enquiries and customer communication. The studio address is First Floor Unit, Prosperous Road, Clane, Co. Kildare, W91 AV2C, Ireland.

Call 089 200 4741 ↗

Using the website

This website does not provide user accounts, a contact form or an online payment facility. It does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The site remembers a language you choose manually and your privacy choices in browser local storage. These settings are not an enquiry or booking record.

When a website is accessed over the internet, the connection necessarily involves technical information such as an IP address and the requested page. This policy does not claim that no technical processing takes place. Details of browser storage and the optional map are explained in our Cookies Policy.

Read the Cookies Policy ↗

Enquiries by phone or WhatsApp

If you call or send a WhatsApp message, the studio may receive your phone number, display name and the information you choose to share. That information is used to respond to you, discuss a service you request and manage related communication. It is not collected through a website form. Please share only the information needed for your enquiry.

Responding to an enquiry or arranging a requested service may be necessary to take steps towards providing that service. Routine customer communication may also be handled for the studio’s legitimate interest in answering and managing enquiries. Where a particular activity requires consent, that choice will be requested separately.

Booksy and other external services

The Booksy button opens the salon’s booking profile. Bookings and information submitted there are handled in Booksy’s systems. WhatsApp and Instagram links open those services. Their own privacy policies govern how they operate, including any processing outside Ireland or the European Economic Area. Following a link does not submit a message or booking automatically.

Google Maps is optional. The embedded map is not loaded until you allow it. Loading it connects your browser to Google and may involve Google’s own cookies or similar technologies. You can keep the map disabled and use the written address or a separate directions link instead.

Keeping information

Browser preferences remain on your device until you change them, clear the site’s data or your browser removes them. Enquiry information should be kept only while needed to answer and manage the matter, and where necessary to meet applicable obligations or resolve a dispute. The period depends on the enquiry; no fixed period is claimed here for every type of customer communication.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, limit or object to processing, or receive certain information in a portable form. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. To raise a privacy question or make a request, contact Aneta by phone or WhatsApp on +353 89 200 4741.

Use Cookie settings in the footer to change the optional map choice at any time. Turning it off removes the embedded map from this site; cookies already placed by Google may also need to be removed using your browser’s controls.

You may also contact Ireland’s Data Protection Commission about a data-protection concern. Data Protection Commission ↗
